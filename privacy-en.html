Privacy policy

Your practice data
belongs to you.

Effective August 31, 2026, and last updated September 3, 2026. Meow Metronome is a local-first practice utility. This policy explains what the current version processes and how you can manage or delete that data.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Scope and developer

This policy applies to Meow Metronome for iOS and iPadOS. The developer is Yuhang Hui. For privacy questions, email yuhang95@foxmail.com. The app does not require an account operated by the developer.

The app does not connect to a server operated by the developer. Network access is limited to optional iCloud sync through Apple CloudKit, retrieving products and verifying membership through StoreKit and the App Store, and opening email or legal web pages when you choose those actions.

Data stored on your device

To provide the metronome, practice history, and statistics, the app stores settings such as BPM, meter, sound, and volume; randomly generated record identifiers; practice start and end times; record source and last-modified time; and state needed to recover an interrupted session. Sync enablement state, the most recent sync time, record count, and sync diagnostics are also stored locally. The developer does not automatically receive this data.

iCloud sync

After you choose to enable sync in Settings and obtain Meow Metronome SUPER access, the app stores record identifiers, start and end times, record source, last-modified time, and deletion markers in your private Apple CloudKit database. This supports sync and conflict merging across devices signed in to the same Apple Account, and restoration after reinstalling the app.

Without a valid entitlement, the sync entry shows a switch and attempting to turn it on opens the SUPER subscription page. With a valid entitlement, the entry shows a disclosure arrow. Settings also includes Manage Practice Data, which is available without SUPER. The detail shows sync status and logs and offers on-device JSON export, merged on-device and iCloud export, deletion of iCloud data only, and deletion of all on-device and iCloud practice data. Ending or losing membership stops future automatic reads and writes but does not automatically delete local or existing iCloud data.

The data counts against your iCloud storage. The developer does not maintain a server containing these records and cannot browse your private database in CloudKit Console.

Purchases and membership

Apple processes purchases, renewals, refunds, and purchase restoration through the App Store. The app reads Apple-verified product and entitlement status at runtime. The developer does not receive card numbers, payment credentials, or complete Apple Account information.

Feedback you choose to send

The Feedback and Privacy Question actions open the system email experience. The developer receives your sender address, message, and any information you choose to attach only after you send an email. This information is used only to reply and handle the request, not for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

Correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to complete the conversation, handle related follow-up, or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by emailing the same address, except where retention is legally required.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current version contains no third-party advertising, cross-app or cross-site tracking, third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, data brokers, or sale of user data. The app does not automatically collect your name, email address, contacts, location, advertising identifier, or device identifier. System diagnostics or app analytics you separately choose to share with Apple are processed independently by Apple.

Service providers and safeguards

Meow Metronome uses Apple CloudKit, StoreKit, and App Store services. If you send an email, your chosen outgoing email provider and the developer’s mailbox provider also process that message. A legal website processes a request when you open it. Apple services are governed by the Apple Privacy Policy and applicable agreements; email and web services are governed by their own privacy policies and applicable law.

The developer does not sell user data or provide it to these services for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking. Providers selected by the developer may process data only as needed to deliver the relevant function and must provide protection comparable to or stronger than this policy. If a future version adds another provider that can access user data, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before that provider is enabled.

Retention and deletion

Local practice records remain until you delete the corresponding individual record in Practice History, delete all practice data in the app, or uninstall the app. When you delete one record, its start time, end time, and source are removed. The app retains only its random identifier and deletion time as a deletion marker so another device does not restore the deleted record. Deletion markers do not expire automatically and are not used for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

From My, choose Manage Practice Data. You can export on-device data as JSON while offline; export merged on-device and iCloud data when online and signed in to the corresponding Apple Account; delete practice data from the current iCloud account while keeping on-device records; or delete practice records, deletion markers, and sync state from this device and the current iCloud account. Export and deletion do not require SUPER. Deleting all data cannot be undone.

Deleting only iCloud data turns off sync. Turning sync on again may upload records retained on this or another device. After deleting all data on this device and in iCloud, other devices may still retain local copies. You can also delete cloud data through system Apple Account and iCloud storage management; exact menu names may vary by system version. System-level cloud deletion does not delete local records on each device. The app stops the current sync when it detects system-level cloud deletion.

The developer cannot remotely browse or delete records in your private CloudKit database, but can provide system-setting instructions by email. Because there is no developer-operated account, there is no separate app account to delete.

Your choices

You may use local features without buying SUPER; try to enable member-only sync and decide whether to subscribe when the SUPER page appears; open Manage Practice Data without SUPER to view sync status and logs and export or delete practice data; delete individual records in Practice History; manage or delete this app’s cloud data in system iCloud storage settings; or contact the developer about this policy, deletion of support emails, and data-management guidance.

Policy updates

If data handling materially changes, the developer will update this policy, in-app explanations, and App Store privacy information before enabling the new processing, and will change the date above. Any new processing that requires consent will be presented as a choice before it begins.
